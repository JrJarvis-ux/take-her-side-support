Take Her Side Support

For help with the camera, your account, partner linking or Pro, email TakeHerSideSupport@gmail.com.

Include your app version, iPhone model and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords, payment details or private photos.

Camera access

Open iPhone Settings → Apps → Take Her Side to allow Camera access. Photo Library access is requested when importing or saving photos.

Partner linking and Live Director

Both partners need their own accounts. Create a pairing code in the app and enter it on the other phone. For Live Director, keep both iPhones on the same Wi-Fi with Bluetooth enabled, and allow Local Network access. Watching starts only when the photographer allows it.

Pro purchases

Use Restore purchases in the Pro screen to check purchases on your Apple Account. Monthly and annual Pro can cover one linked partner after Apple verification and account association. Partner access ends when you unlink.

Manage or cancel renewals in Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app or an app account does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, visit Apple’s refund service.

Privacy and account deletion

Settings → Privacy provides local deletion and cloud consent controls. Signed-in users can delete their account from Settings. If you cannot sign in, contact support from the email associated with your account.
