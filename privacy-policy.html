Take Her Side

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Take Her Side helps couples take photos with real-time guidance. Camera guidance and editing work on your phone. Optional partner sharing, account sync and cloud advice handle data as described below.

Who we are. Take Her Side (“the app”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is operated by Lee Jarvis, trading as TakeHerSide.co, based in England, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the purposes of UK data protection law. You can reach us any time at TakeHerSideSupport@gmail.com.

1. Our core principle: on-device first

Local camera guidance and editing work without uploading your photos:

  • Framing, pose and lighting guidance, object/scene suggestions, filters and photo enhancement run locally. The app does not identify people.
  • Local face and pose measurements stay on your device. A photo or frame you choose to send for cloud advice may contain a visible face or body.
  • Images leave your device when you choose partner sharing, allow your linked partner to watch the camera, export through another app, or separately consent to cloud image advice.

2. Information handled entirely on your device

Your original photos, local editing results and detection measurements stay on your phone unless you choose to share or export images. Signing in syncs preference and shoot metadata as described in section 3. You can erase local data from Settings → Privacy → Delete everything:

  • Photos you take, import, or save.
  • Live camera frames and any on-device face/pose detection results.
  • Your original Inspiration Library images. Preference and library metadata may sync when signed in.
  • Your album, saved shoots, and progress.

Microphone & speech

If you ask the coach a question by voice, speech is recognised on your device and turned into text. Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to us.

Location

If you enable it, the app uses your approximate location only to suggest nearby photo spots while you’re using it. Your location is never stored on our servers and never shared.

3. Information we do process (Couple Link)

Couple Link is the optional account that lets two partners pair up and shoot each other with each person’s saved preferences. If you create an account, we process:

WhatWhy
Email address and display nameTo create and secure your account and show who you are to your partner.
Sign-in identifier from Apple or Google (if you use them)To let you sign in without a password. We receive a token, not your Apple/Google password.
Couple Link pairing (who you’re linked with)To connect you and your partner.
Your Muse profile, preferences, and Muse Library metadata, plus saved shoot “memory” metadataSo your preferences follow you across your own devices and are available to your partner’s app when they photograph you. This is preference data — not your photos.
A push notification tokenTo notify you when your partner sends you a shoot plan. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

Shared photos are end-to-end encrypted

Partner photo sharing encrypts images on your device before upload, using device-held keys. We store encrypted images and cannot decrypt them. Deleting local keys removes this device’s ability to decrypt its copies; it does not erase copies your partner has already saved. Live Director sends reduced preview frames directly to your paired partner’s phone over an encrypted local connection while you explicitly allow watching.

4. Optional features that are off by default

These are disabled unless you turn them on in Settings:

  • Local diagnostics. If enabled, this phone stores session-level summaries such as shot counts and scores. These counters are not uploaded automatically.
  • Smarter coach (cloud). The coach works fully on your device by default. If you enable the cloud coach, only your typed question and a short line of text describing the current shot are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate an answer. Your photos, your face, and any image data are never sent.

5. Optional cloud images and purchases

Optional cloud image advice

With separate consent, a resized photo with location and camera metadata removed is sent through our servers to Google Gemini for photo advice. Generated example pictures send the selected photo and approved suggestions to an image model; they are labelled AI examples and may alter details. Optional live scene advice sends occasional reduced camera frames. Cloud images are encrypted in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted: the provider must process the image. Live scene frames are not stored by our application. Stored input and example images expire within 24 hours and are queued for deletion; failed deletion attempts are retried. Deleting advice or withdrawing consent requests deletion sooner. Provider processing is subject to the provider’s retention and security terms.

Cloud requests also use account or anonymous user identifiers, consent receipts, job status and quota counters to secure access and limit usage. Operational logs contain identifiers and status codes, not image bytes or raw prompts.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. We do not receive payment card details. To provide shared Pro access, we verify Apple-signed monthly/annual purchase records, associate the subscription with your account and check current Apple status. One linked partner can receive access while the subscription and link remain active. After account deletion, a minimal transaction ownership record remains without your account identifier to prevent an old receipt being claimed again.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not show third-party advertising or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • We do not perform facial recognition or identify individuals.

6. Legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — to provide the account, Couple Link, syncing, and encrypted sharing you ask for.
  • Consent — for optional features you switch on (diagnostics, cloud coach, location, notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings or your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure and working, and to respond to your support requests.

8. International transfers

Some of these providers process data on servers outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum or equivalent contractual protections) to keep your information protected.

9. How long we keep information

  • On-device information stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account and synced information is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed (see below).
  • Encrypted shared photos remain until you or your partner delete them, or you delete your account.

10. Your rights and choices

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email TakeHerSideSupport@gmail.com. Two rights are built directly into the app:

  • Delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) permanently deletes your account and all associated data — your profile, Couple Link, synced preferences, encrypted shared photos, and everything on this device. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete everything on this device (Settings → Privacy → Delete everything) erases all local data and signs you out.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

11. Security

We protect your information with end-to-end encryption for shared photos, encryption in transit (TLS), and database access rules that limit each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we design the app to hold as little of your data as possible in the first place.

12. Children

Take Her Side is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app asks you to confirm your age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Your continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
